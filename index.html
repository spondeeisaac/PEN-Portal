Install PEN Portal on iPhone

On iPhone, installation is done through Safari.

1. Open PEN Portal in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
4. Tap Add. PEN Portal will appear on the Home Screen like an app.
